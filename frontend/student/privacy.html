Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

This website provides practice tests and preparation materials. It is not the official EPS-TOPIK registration, examination, or results service.

Information used by the platform

  • Account information: your name and email address. The password you create is sent to the service for account authentication and stored as a password hash.
  • Study information: exam sets you access, answers you submit, and practice results, so the platform can run tests and show your progress.
  • Sign-in information: the site stores an authentication token in your browser to keep your account signed in. Password reset uses your email address and a verification code.
  • Contact messages: if you use the website contact form, your name, email address, subject, and message are stored in the administrator portal so the team can respond.

How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, provide practice tests, save answers and results, manage access to test sets, and respond to contact messages.

WhatsApp purchases

If you choose to contact the administrator from checkout, the prepared WhatsApp message includes the test sets you selected and the total shown on the checkout page. You decide whether to send it. WhatsApp is operated separately, and its own privacy terms apply. Do not send passwords, one-time codes (OTPs), card PINs, or full card details in chat.

Storage and service providers

Account and practice information is processed by the platform's hosting, database, and email services as needed to operate the site and account features. During registration, your email address is also checked by ZeroBounce to determine whether the mailbox can receive email before a verification code is sent. Information sent through WhatsApp is handled by WhatsApp and the administrator. The platform does not use the checkout page to collect card numbers or card security codes.

Requests about your information

To ask a question or request correction or deletion of account information, contact the platform administrator. We may need to retain some records where required to provide the service or meet legal and security obligations.

Contact

Contact the platform administrator on WhatsApp.
